Generic Office Productivity Suite
Functional Architecture & Requirements Specification
Deconstructed Architectural Requirements Covering Document Processing, Spreadsheet Calculation, Presentation Graphics, and Freeform Note-Taking Subsystems
Executive Scope & Overview:
This document establishes a complete, industrial-grade functional requirements specification for a generic office productivity engine. The specifications are decomposed into four core application domains: Word Processing, Spreadsheet Analysis, Presentation Authoring, and Unbounded Digital Notebooks. All user interface surface metaphors (such as toolbars, ribbons, and tabs) have been abstracted into pure, reusable architectural capabilities, domain engines, data pipelines, and UI-agnostic system requirements.
